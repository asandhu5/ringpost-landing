A no-show costs more than the appointment itself. You held the slot, turned other people away for it, maybe prepared for it. Then it sits empty, too late to fill.

Most no-shows aren't rude. People forget, their plans change, or they meant to cancel and never got round to it because cancelling meant a phone call during working hours.

## Reminders work because they remove friction

A reminder does two jobs. It jogs the memory, and it gives the customer an easy moment to say "actually, I can't make it." That second job is the important one. A cancellation the day before is a slot you can still fill. A no-show isn't.

## What makes a good reminder

- **The right timing.** Close enough to the appointment that it's relevant, early enough that a cancelled slot can still be rebooked.
- **The right channel.** Most people read a text within minutes. A reminder they never see doesn't help.
- **The details.** What, when, where, and with whom. "See you tomorrow at 10:30 for your boiler service" is better than "you have an appointment."
- **An easy way out.** Let people reply to reschedule or cancel. If changing a booking takes a phone call during your busiest hours, many people won't do it. They just won't come.

## What happens after the reply matters too

If a customer replies "can we make it Thursday instead?", someone has to check the calendar, offer a time, move the booking and confirm. If that waits until you're free, the reschedule can turn into a no-show anyway.

## Deposits and cancellation policies

For longer or high-value appointments, many businesses ask for a deposit or set a cancellation window, such as 24 hours' notice. These work best when they're clear from the start: mentioned when the booking is made, repeated in the reminder, and applied fairly. Customers rarely mind a policy they were told about. They mind one that comes as a surprise.

## Keep a short waiting list

When a slot opens up the day before, someone who wanted that time is often happy to take it. Note down customers who asked for a time you didn't have. A cancellation then becomes a quick message to them instead of an empty chair.
