---
title: "Before you retry a social post, check the platform"
description: "How to avoid publishing the same content twice after a connection interruption."
date: 2026-11-06
---

## A lost response can leave an uncertain result

Sometimes a platform accepts a post while the connection closes before the confirmation reaches your dashboard. The content may already be live. An uncertain delivery should be checked before another attempt.

## Check the original destination

Open the connected social account and look for the caption and media. Check scheduled content as well as the public feed. If you find the post, keep the original rather than creating a duplicate. If it is absent, return to the dashboard and review the failure details.

## Keep a clear publishing routine

Choose the destination accounts deliberately, review the caption for each platform and track delivery in the dashboard. When someone else manages your social accounts too, agree who will resolve uncertain deliveries. That small habit keeps retries from becoming duplicate posts.
