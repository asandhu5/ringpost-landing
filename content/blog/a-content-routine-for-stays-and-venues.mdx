## Start with the experience

A room is more than a bed. Guests want to understand the light, the arrival, the surroundings and the feeling of spending time there. Begin with your own photographs of the spaces you actually offer. Make a short list of the questions guests ask before booking: parking, arrival times, accessibility and what is included. Keep those answers in your business knowledge so your captions and customer replies use the same facts.

## Build a small weekly rhythm

Choose one space, one detail and one useful answer each week. A walkthrough can introduce the room; a close-up can show a thoughtful detail; a practical post can explain arrival. Use Content Studio to explore framing and motion, then check that the result accurately represents the property. Never present an invented amenity or a generated view as something a guest will receive.

## Review, then schedule

Read each caption beside the image or video. Check dates, inclusions and any offer conditions. Use Posting to choose your connected platforms and schedule approved content. Keep availability questions tied to your real booking process.
