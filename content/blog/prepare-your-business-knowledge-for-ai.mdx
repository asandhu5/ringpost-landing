## Start with decisions customers make

List your services or products with the correct names, prices and any conditions. A price from a quotation is different from a fixed price. A deposit is different from the full amount. Make those distinctions explicit in your business knowledge. Add opening hours, special closures and how to reach your team.

## Make exceptions easy to find

Write down cancellation rules, booking requirements and the situations your team should handle. For a repair business this might be a diagnosis or a custom estimate. For a clinic it includes clinical questions. An assistant should explain the administrative process without guessing a professional answer.

## Test the awkward questions

Try a missing price, a holiday, a corrected date and a request for a discount. Check that the assistant keeps unknown information unknown and follows your handoff preferences. Repeat these checks when important business details change.
